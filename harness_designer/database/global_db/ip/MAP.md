## FILES

#### __init__.py

Package init for `ip`; no real logic of its own. IP (ingress protection) rating tables are split into their own subdirectory because an IP rating is composed of separate fluid/solid/supplementary ratings rather than one flat lookup.

#### fluid.py

IP fluid-ingress rating table.

#### solid.py

IP solid-ingress rating table.

#### supp.py

IP supplementary rating table.
