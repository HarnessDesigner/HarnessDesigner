# harness_designer/ray_tracing/bvh_processor.py

Rewritten 2026-11-13. The earlier thread-pool processor (`BVHWorkerThread`, `ThreadedBVHProcessor`) is gone; `build_bvh(vertices, faces)` is now a 20-line wrapper that makes the arrays contiguous (`np.ascontiguousarray`, a copy only if they are not) and calls `bvh.calculate_centroids_parallel` and `FastBVHBuilder(...).build()`. `LEAF_THRESHOLD = 4`. The cost is the compiled builder (see `bvh.md`). A larger leaf threshold (8-16) builds a smaller tree and a faster build, and traverses more triangles per leaf; the right value depends on the GPU and needs a measurement on a real scene.
