# harness_designer/ui/widgets/autocomplete_combobox.py

AutoCompleteComboBox -- an editable QComboBox with its own inline QCompleter and a mirrored choice list (SetItems/Append/Insert/Delete/Clear keep the list and the completer in step; Qt's own clear()/addItems() do NOT feed the completer). Used by ui/prop_ctrls/combobox_prop.py, color_prop.py, ComboBoxCtrl and ui/toolbar/snap_angle_button.py.

Cold path (192 lines): the AST pass finds no per-frame or per-event handlers, no nested loops, no loops that allocate Qt or NumPy objects, no timers and no threads. It runs when its feature is opened or used, so there is nothing here worth optimising.
